# ZManager Desktop Microsoft Store Privacy Statement

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

This statement applies to the Microsoft Store edition of ZManager Desktop. That
edition uses the local profile: its archive operations run on your device, and
it does not include TZAP hosted sign-in, certificate enrollment, contact sync,
or hosted certificate-status checks. This statement does not cover other
distributions that include online account features or visits to the tzap.org
website.

## Who publishes the app

Frank Zhu publishes ZManager Desktop under the tzap name. For privacy questions
about information you choose to send us, contact [support@tzap.org](mailto:support@tzap.org).

## Information the app processes

### Archive files and passwords

When you open, list, test, create, or extract an archive, ZManager processes the
selected files and the names, paths, metadata, and contents needed for that
operation. This processing takes place on your device. The Store edition does
not upload your archives or their contents to TZAP services.

Passwords you enter for archive operations are used to perform the requested
operation. They are kept in application memory as needed and are not saved as
preferences or included in diagnostic logs.

### App data, identities, and contacts

The app stores preferences and information needed for features you choose,
including local identity, certificate, and contact records. Private keys used
for local signing remain on your device; the app uses the operating system's
secure credential storage for secret material where supported. This Store
edition does not transmit those records to TZAP hosted account services.

### Local device sharing

If you choose to share files with another device, ZManager discovers and
connects to participating devices on your local network and sends the items you
select to the recipient you choose. Those devices and the local network carry
the transfer. It is not an upload to TZAP cloud storage.

### Diagnostic logs

ZManager writes diagnostic logs on your device. They can contain event times,
session and process identifiers, feature names, and short status or error
details. The logger filters fields identified as paths or credentials, but you
should still review a log before sharing it. Logs rotate locally when they
reach 4 MiB, with one previous log retained. The app does not automatically
send logs or crash reports to TZAP. If you choose to copy and email a log to
support, the information in it is received with your message.

## How information is used and shared

The app uses locally processed information to carry out the actions you
request, save your preferences, and show operation status. It does not include
advertising, product analytics, or automatic diagnostic uploads.

ZManager does not send the information described above to TZAP hosted services
in this Store edition. When you choose local sharing or contact support, the
recipient you select receives the information you send. Separately, Microsoft
may process information about Store transactions, licensing, downloads, and
updates under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/privacy/privacystatement).

## Storage and retention

Files and archives remain in the locations you choose on your device.
Preferences, local identity and contact records, and diagnostic logs are stored
in ZManager's local app data. You can manage or remove that data on your device.
Diagnostic logs rotate as described above. ZManager cannot retrieve or delete
files or app data stored only on your device.

If you email support, we use your message and reply address to respond to your
request. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the
request and maintain necessary support records, then delete it unless we must
keep it longer to meet a legal obligation. We do not use support messages for
marketing.

## Your choices and requests

You choose which files to open or create and whether to share files with a
nearby device. You can inspect and remove app data and diagnostic logs on your
device. Because this Store edition does not send archive contents, local
identity records, or contacts to TZAP, we cannot access or delete that local
information for you.

To ask about information you sent to support, email [support@tzap.org](mailto:support@tzap.org).
Please do not email archive passwords, private keys, or archive contents.

## Children

ZManager is a general-purpose archive utility and is not designed to collect
personal information from children. The Store edition does not create a TZAP
hosted account or send app-use information to TZAP.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this statement will be updated and its
“Last updated” date will change. The statement describes the Microsoft Store
edition identified above.
