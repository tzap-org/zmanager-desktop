## Information we process

### Files and archive information

ZManager is an archive utility. When you open, inspect, test, create, or extract
an archive, the app processes the file paths, names, metadata, and contents
needed to carry out that operation. These operations run on your device. The
app does not upload the archives you open or create to TZAP hosted account
services.

Passwords and passphrases you enter for archive operations are used to perform
the requested operation. They are not included in diagnostic logs or saved as
preferences.

If you choose a file-sharing action, the selected items are transferred to the
recipient device you choose over your local network. The recipient then has a
copy of the items you sent. This transfer is not an upload to ZManager or TZAP
cloud storage.

### Information stored on your device

ZManager stores application preferences, account and certificate metadata,
contact records you import or sync, and other information needed for the
features you use. Where supported, signing credentials are stored using your
operating system's credential protection. Private signing keys are used on
your device and are not sent to TZAP hosted services as part of certificate
enrollment; the service receives the enrollment request and public certificate
information needed to issue or manage a certificate.

The app also writes diagnostic logs on your device. These can contain event
times, app and runtime information, operation names, and status or error
details. Logs are not automatically uploaded. You can inspect or copy the
diagnostic information from the app; review it before sharing because it may
describe actions you took.

### Online features in other distributions

The Microsoft Store package uses the **local** profile. It does not include
hosted sign-in or contact-sync features and does not send account requests to
TZAP hosted services. Other ZManager distributions may offer an **online**
profile. If you use one of those builds and choose to sign in or use a hosted
account feature, it communicates with TZAP services, currently at
`login.tzap.org` and `sign.tzap.org`. Depending on the feature, this can
involve:

- account and sign-in information handled by the hosted sign-in flow;
- session and authorization data, stored locally in the operating system's
  credential store and sent to the service when needed;
- device identifiers and certificate signing requests or public certificate
  details for certificate enrollment, renewal, or lifecycle management;
- contact-card or public-certificate information needed to sync contacts and
  check certificate status; and
- ordinary network request data, such as your IP address, request time, and
  technical client information, which may be processed by the service
  infrastructure.

When an online build opens a browser or an external identity provider, that
provider processes information under its own privacy statement.

## How we use information

We use information processed by ZManager to:

- open, create, verify, extract, and share archives when you request those
  actions;
- save your preferences and local identity information;
- authenticate your optional hosted account and provide the account features
  you request;
- enroll or manage certificates and verify certificate status when you request
  those features; and
- diagnose and fix problems using local diagnostic information you choose to
  share with us.

ZManager does not include advertising, personalized advertising, or product
analytics. We do not use ZManager file contents or diagnostic logs to train
generative AI models.

## When information is disclosed

We do not sell your personal information. We disclose information only as
needed to provide a feature you request, to protect the service, or to comply
with law. In particular:

- TZAP hosted services receive the account and certificate information
  described above when you use their online features.
- A recipient receives files you deliberately send using local-device
  sharing.
- Microsoft processes information associated with Microsoft Store downloads,
  licensing, updates, and your Microsoft account under its own
  [privacy statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/privacy/privacystatement).
- Your operating system and any external identity provider may process
  information under their own privacy terms.

## Storage, security, and retention

Files, preferences, account records, certificates, and diagnostic logs are
stored locally on your device unless you choose an online account feature or
send files to another device. You control the files you open and create.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information
handled by the app and its hosted connections. Online connections use HTTPS.
No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely
secure.

## Your choices and requests

You choose whether to use optional online account features. You can sign out
and remove locally stored account information through the app's account
controls. You can manage or delete files, preferences, and other local data on
your device. Diagnostic logs are not sent unless you choose to share them.

To ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information that we control,
contact **[privacy contact email]**. We may need to verify your request and may
retain information where the law permits or requires it. Requests about data
held by Microsoft Store, an identity provider, or another service must be
directed to that provider.
